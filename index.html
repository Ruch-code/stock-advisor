2 · KYC — 5–15 minute eKYC

  1. PAN card — must match the bank account name
  2. Aadhaar card — OTP-based eKYC
  3. Bank account — IFSC + cancelled-cheque photo
  4. Selfie / liveness check
  5. Signature on a white paper

The order of these buckets is the single biggest edge most people ignore: build Core before chasing narrative coins.

4 · Tax — the silent 31% eroder

Every sale: 30% flat tax + 1% TDS on each transaction (Sec 115BBH / 194S). No loss set-off. No 80C relief. It is the same 30% whether you held 1 day or 5 years.

✔Don't churn. Every round-trip burns ~31%+ — traders who scalp lose to the tax, not the market.
✔Don't sell to "rebalance". Prefer yield (Aave/Ledger) or borrow against BTC instead of selling into tax.
✔Hold in self-custody ≥ 1 year — transfers between your own wallets are not taxable events.
✘P2P cash deals you can't prove = gift of a tax problem. Keep exchange trails.

File Schedule VDA in your ITR. If you hold long-term and never sell, the 30% only ever touches what you actually realize.

5 · How traders lose money (the 4 traps)

Timing trap — buying after a green candle you saw on a reel. Fear & Greed is screaming; you are the exit liquidity.
Fix: fixed DCA on set dates, never lump-sum into green streaks. Buy when -60% from its own ATH, not +60% from it.
Charges trap — 0.1% fee per side ×2 + spread + ~31% tax per round-trip means a "profitable" 10% trade can actually net negative.
Fix: 5–10 trades a year, not 5–10 a day. Use limit orders; batch buys.
Knowledge trap — buying an altcoin because of a logo. You don't know what backs it, who controls it, or why it moves.
Fix: BTC & ETH first. A narrative coin is a ≤5% lottery ticket, bought only when deeply beaten down.
Security trap — keeping everything on the exchange, SMS 2FA, seed phrase on a screenshot.
Fix: exchange = on-ramp only. Hardware wallet above ₹50K. Authenticator-app 2FA. Seed on metal.

6 · Quick start (15 minutes)

  1. Create Binance → enable INR P2P/UPI deposit
  2. eKYC: PAN + Aadhaar OTP + selfie + bank IFSC
  3. Deposit ₹1,000 → buy ₹500 BTC + ₹500 ETH
  4. Set a weekly auto-buy (SIP) ₹500–2,000 on fixed dates
  5. Own key: hardware wallet (Ledger ~₹7,999) above ₹50K
  6. Transfer to self-custody — exchange stays an on-ramp only
  7. Track in CoinStats/Delta; file ITR with Schedule VDA
